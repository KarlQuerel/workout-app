Workout - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Workout is an offline gym tracker for Android. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting SDK. It does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Data you create

Your workout data (sessions, exercises, weights, repetitions, and history) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded. You can export it to a CSV file yourself, and that file goes only where you choose to put it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: to show the rest countdown and the alarm when a rest period ends.
  • Vibration: to buzz when a rest period ends.
  • Exact alarms: so the rest timer fires on time when the screen is locked.

None of these permissions are used to collect information about you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.
